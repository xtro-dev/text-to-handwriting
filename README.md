# Inkwell — Text to Handwriting Studio

A free, client-side web application that converts typed or pasted text into realistic handwritten pages with natural character variation, customizable paper styles, automatic pagination, and high-resolution PDF, PNG, and JPEG export.

## Features

- **Realistic Handwriting Engine**: 15 locally bundled open-source handwriting fonts with per-character micro-rotation, baseline wave drift, margin wobble, pen thickness physics, and ink pressure variation.
- **Optional Humanize Handwriting Mode**: Toggleable human imperfection mode (OFF by default) with `Low` / `Medium` / `High` intensity, deterministic pen strike-throughs, small above/beside corrections, overwritten letters, spacing/baseline variation, and subtle notebook-line drift — preserved cleanly across live preview and PDF/image exports.
- **9 Paper Styles**: Ruled Notebook, College Ruled, Wide Ruled, Legal Pad, Blank White, Grid Paper, Dotted Journal, Plain Cream, and Old Parchment.
- **Full Page & Document Controls**: A4, A5, Letter, and Legal formats (Portrait & Landscape), custom colors, margins, alignment, paragraph spacing, indentation, title, date, header, footer, and page numbering.
- **Free High-Resolution Exports**: Multi-page PDF, individual PNG/JPEG images, and multi-page ZIP downloads with zero watermarks or limits.
